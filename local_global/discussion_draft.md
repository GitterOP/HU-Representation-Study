# Draft discussion: connecting global, local, and endmember preservation

We re-evaluated the saved outputs of all six systems using identical, method-independent reference masks. Boundary bands were obtained from four-neighbor changes in the dominant reference material followed by a one-pixel dilation. Mixed pixels had maximum reference abundance below 0.8. Endmembers were matched one-to-one by minimum total spectral angle, and the same permutation was used for abundance evaluation.

In Samson, DINO achieved the lowest global reconstruction RMSE (0.0995). The lowest boundary reconstruction error was obtained by DINO (0.1249), whereas boundary abundance error was lowest for DINO (0.1061). The best mean endmember SAD was obtained by AE-Patch (2.79 degrees); the global reconstruction winner ranked 4 on this endmember criterion.

In Jasper Ridge, MAE achieved the lowest global reconstruction RMSE (0.0496). The lowest boundary reconstruction error was obtained by MAE (0.0596), whereas boundary abundance error was lowest for MAE (0.1115). The best mean endmember SAD was obtained by MAE (7.08 degrees); the global reconstruction winner ranked 1 on this endmember criterion.

In Urban, DINO achieved the lowest global reconstruction RMSE (0.1007). The lowest boundary reconstruction error was obtained by DINO (0.0978), whereas boundary abundance error was lowest for DINO (0.1768). The best mean endmember SAD was obtained by DINO (6.94 degrees); the global reconstruction winner ranked 1 on this endmember criterion.

The global reconstruction winner also minimized boundary and mixed-pixel reconstruction error in 3/3 and 3/3 scenes, respectively, under the primary masks. However, it minimized reconstruction error in only 4/11 dominant-material regions, individual endmember SAD in 3/11 material-scene combinations, and individual material abundance RMSE in 6/11 combinations. These are descriptive counts, not independent statistical replicates.

For example, MAE had the lowest global reconstruction error in Jasper Ridge, while PCA had the lowest reconstruction error within road-dominant pixels (0.0761 versus 0.0789). In Urban, the global winner was DINO, while AE-Patch had the lowest reconstruction error in roof-dominant pixels (0.1037 versus 0.1315). In Samson, DINO led global reconstruction, while AE-Patch had the lowest mean endmember SAD. Thus, scene-level criteria can mask material-specific differences. Under the stricter mixed-pixel sensitivity mask in Jasper Ridge, the best local reconstruction was obtained by PCA.

These comparisons distinguish agreement in reconstruction rankings from preservation of material signatures and abundances. A low global residual is not by itself a sufficient validation of endmember or abundance accuracy. Region-specific and material-specific metrics should therefore accompany global reconstruction measures. All reconstruction maps share fixed color limits across systems, and abundance maps use a separate shared scale.

The analysis is descriptive: each scene contains six evaluated systems, and the spatial pixels are not independent experimental replicates. Boundary widths and mixture thresholds were varied as sensitivity checks. These observations do not isolate architectural or training mechanisms. Low-signal masks are brightness proxies and cannot establish shadow.

Metric clarification: the existing CSV field rRMSE contains conventional reconstruction RMSE, whereas relative RMSE is a separate norm ratio. The existing abundance aRMSE aggregates by averaging pixelwise RMSE rather than taking a pooled square root. The manuscript terminology and equations should be reconciled with these definitions.

Reference consistency is an additional limitation. Under the scales loaded by the repository, the product of reference abundances and reference signatures does not exactly reproduce the observed cube. Its reconstruction RMSE is 0.3678 in Samson, 0.0469 in Jasper Ridge, and 0.0848 in Urban. No scale correction was fitted or applied. Consequently, disagreements among reconstruction, spectral-angle, and abundance criteria cannot be attributed solely to representation learning; they are conditional on the benchmark reference conventions. Spectral-angle fidelity does not by itself establish radiometric amplitude fidelity.
