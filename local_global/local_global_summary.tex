Figure~\ref{fig:local_global_summary} contrasts scene-level reconstruction with material-specific preservation. In Urban, DINO leads globally, whereas AE-Patch achieves the lowest local RMSE in roof-dominant pixels. In Samson, AE-Patch has the lowest mean endmember SAD, despite DINO achieving the lowest global RMSE. Thus, global reconstruction quality alone does not establish uniform preservation of every material region and endmember.
